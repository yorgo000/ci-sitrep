# Corrections

Errors and omissions in the CI SITREP are corrected in public, on this page, newest first. A correction fixes the report and the dashboard together — the entry below says what was wrong, and what it is now. If you find an error or a material omission, report it to [ci-sitrep@confluence1.com](mailto:ci-sitrep@confluence1.com).

- **25 Nov 2026 — Correction: "AI now writes the majority of new code" overstated the evidence.** The claim rested on Google's 75% figure for its own new code (AI-generated, engineer-approved); the two industry-wide surveys are below half — JetBrains ~47% of code written fully by AI agents, Sonar 42% of committed code AI-generated/assisted (65% is Sonar's projection for 2027, not a current figure). Now stated as "close to half of new code — three-quarters at Google" in [the article](index.html#ai-writes-close-to-half-of-new-code-three-quarters-at) and the situation summary.
- **19 Nov 2026 — Omission: Strands Agents was missing from the report.** AWS's open-source agent stack — the Strands Agents SDK, Strands harness, and Strands Box sandbox — had no coverage at all. Added after a reader pointed out the gap: [the Strands harness article](index.html#awss-open-source-agent-harness-runs-the-same-models-for-28), [the Strands Box article](index.html#awss-agent-sandbox-enforces-rules-the-agent-cannot-see), and [the CVE-2026-115415 article](index.html#a-prompt-could-switch-off-secret-masking-in-a-strands-tool).
- **19 Nov 2026 — Correction: the 109:1 machine-to-human identity ratio was attributed to SailPoint.** It is Palo Alto Networks' 2026 Identity Security Landscape finding (109 machine identities per human, 79 of them AI agents). Re-attributed in [the article](index.html#for-every-human-identity-enterprises-now-manage-109-machine) and in the dashboard figure and its time series.
- **18 Nov 2026 — Correction: Black Duck OSSRA — 97% of organisations use AI coding assistants was wrong.** The figure is 67%. The 97% is the share using open-source AI models — a different number from the same report. Corrected in [the article](index.html#mean-vulnerabilities-per-codebase-581-up-107-year-on-year) and the dashboard.
- **18 Nov 2026 — Correction: CodeRabbit — 2.74× is the maximum across vulnerability categories, not the overall rate.** Now stated as "up to 2.74×" everywhere, including [the article](index.html#ai-code-carries-up-to-274-the-vulnerabilities-of-human-code), the dashboard, and the CI vs EI page.
- **18 Nov 2026 — Correction: FCA CP26/38 comments close Thu 24 Dec 2026, not Wed.** The weekday was wrong in the dashboard countdown, the report, and the roadmap. Corrected in all three — see [the countdown card](index.html#cd-fca-cp2638-comments-close).
