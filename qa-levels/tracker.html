The four stages

1. Shipyard Boom (Installation) — the build-out surge: capability scales fast, spending runs ahead of returns.

2. Governance Tax (Scanning & Guardrails) — controls and guardrails are bolted on; deployment slows while the tax is paid.

3. The Shakeout (Consolidation) — the field consolidates; weak players and thin wrappers are squeezed out.

4. True P&L Capture (Deployment ROI) — deployment matures and the returns actually land in the P&L.
