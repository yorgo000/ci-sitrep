                                  • 55.6% passed every test while a working exploit succeeded (A.S.E 2.0 / CASE-Bench, arXiv:2611.06294, submitted 4 Nov 2026; preprint, not peer-reviewed)

                                  • A post-hoc review lifts working-and-secure to 47–56% at ~2.5× cost (SusVibes re-run, independent research commissioned by Checkmarx, reported 2 Nov 2026) — [VENDOR-COMMISSIONED]

                                  • Best agentic reviewers catch ~3 in 4 vuln-introducing changes; weakest ~1 in 3 (SecPRBench, arXiv:2610.33509, 24 Oct 2026; preprint, not peer-reviewed)

                                  • Curated security rules cut measured risk in AI-generated code by up to 70% (Secure Code Warrior, 17 Nov 2026, AI Trust Index wave 2)
                                  • Deterministic SAST and AI code review each miss what the other catches — combined they reach 101 of 108 attacker-reachable flaws (Snyk, arXiv:2610.38617, submitted 28 Oct 2026; preprint, not peer-reviewed)
                                  • LLM security repairs pass every validation gate in only 13.5% of requests (SECR-Eval, MDPI *Information* 2026, 17, 1152, 15 Nov 2026; peer-reviewed)

                                  Complementary 01 · J-Curve

                                  The Productivity J-Curve: Measured net returns dip negative early while firms absorb intangible complementary investments—code verification (~38% flaw rate, Veracode Fall 2026), guardrails, and compliance.

                                  Complementary 02 · Diffusion

                                  4. Rogers (1962) / Moore (1991)

                                  Adoption S-Curve & Chasm: Technical diffusion runs fast on existing digital rails, while institutional absorption (liability, auditability, decision rights) runs at human speed (δ, unmeasured).

                                  Complementary 03 · Evolution Axis

                                  5. Simon Wardley (2005 / 2015)

                                  Genesis → Commodity: Compute and foundation models drift toward utility pricing while strategic differentiation shifts upward into semantic data context and verification.

                                  Complementary 04 · Infra-App Loop

                                  6. Chris Dixon (2021)

                                  Infrastructure–Application Loop: Wave 1 infrastructure enables agentic applications that expose reliability gaps, demanding Wave 2 verification, deterministic guardrails, and audit layers.
