# What didn't make it

Every morning we review far more than we publish. Most of what we look at doesn't clear the bar — and until now, what we left out was invisible. This page lists the near-misses: items we considered for the report and decided against, with the reason. Judge for yourself whether we called it right.

Every entry carries one of five reasons — the list is closed:

- **Below the bar** — a single uncorroborated claim, or no methodology disclosed.
- **Already covered** — the report already carries this finding.
- **Superseded** — a stronger source covers the same ground.
- **Out of scope** — not created intelligence in code or finance.
- **Not yet** — plausible, but not verifiable today. These may graduate into the report later.

This list starts 27 November 2026. Earlier decisions weren't recorded — we didn't keep them, so we won't reconstruct them.

If you think we got one wrong, tell us: [ci-sitrep@confluence1.com](mailto:ci-sitrep@confluence1.com).
